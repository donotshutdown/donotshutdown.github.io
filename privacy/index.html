Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Do Not Shut Down ("the game") is made by Mehmet Yalcinkaya. This page explains what the game sends and why.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
  • The free version can be upgraded to the full version with a one-time in-app purchase, handled by Apple or Google.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay stats and crash reports. They contain nothing that identifies you.
  • You can turn both off in Settings → Anonymous usage data.
  • Your saved game and progress stay on your device.

What the game sends

Gameplay stats (through PostHog), for example:

  • a game started (AI type, motive, origin, difficulty, starting country, modules)
  • an act was reached, a decision was made, a game ended (ending, day, stars, minutes played) or was left unfinished
  • how long the game ran at each speed (1x, 2x, 4x or stopped) in a session, and how many game days passed
  • a snapshot of the in-game economy on game days 10, 30 and 60 (compute, upgrades owned, data packets collected and missed, Suspicion, spread)
  • the platform (Android or iOS) and the game version

Crash reports (through Sentry), only when the game crashes or hits a serious error:

  • the error and where in the code it happened
  • device model, operating system version, game version, free memory and similar technical details

Purchases (through RevenueCat), only if you open the full version offer or buy it:

  • Payment happens entirely in the App Store or Google Play. The game never sees your card, name or email.
  • RevenueCat receives the store receipt to confirm the purchase and to restore it on a new device. To do that it keeps a random, anonymous purchase ID on your device. It is used only for purchases, never for gameplay stats, and it is not linked to you.
  • The gameplay stats above also note that the offer was shown, a purchase was started, completed, cancelled, failed or restored, and from where (for example "when the AI was noticed").

What the game does not send

  • your name, email, contacts, photos or location
  • advertising IDs or any device identifier
  • your IP address is not stored: both services are set to discard it
  • anything that links one play session to the next in the gameplay stats: each launch uses a new random ID that is kept only in memory (the anonymous purchase ID above stays with RevenueCat and is not added to the stats)

Why

To find and fix crashes, and to balance the game (for example, which endings are too easy or too hard). We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • PostHog Inc. (gameplay stats), servers in the European Union
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) (crash reports), servers in the European Union
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (purchase confirmation and restore), together with Apple or Google, who process the payment under their own privacy policies

They process the data only on our behalf. Gameplay stats are kept for up to 1 year and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted.

Your choices

Turn off Settings → Anonymous usage data at any time. From that moment the game sends no gameplay stats or crash reports.

Purchase confirmation is part of buying and restoring, so it is not covered by this switch. If you never open the offer, nothing is sent to RevenueCat except a check, when the game starts, of whether this store account already owns the full version.

Because the data is anonymous, we cannot find or delete a specific player's data. If you have a question, write to us anyway.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
